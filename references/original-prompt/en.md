# XXD Shop 003 · Original brief (English reading translation)

> Reading translation only. The canonical runtime prompt is [zh-CN.md](zh-CN.md).

Design a highly recognisable custom-shaped e-commerce tag board / product recommendation board for the current product.

If the user provides a product image, the real product is the core: faithfully keep its shape, colour, packaging, material and key identifying features. If only a product name or category is given, build the corresponding visual from the product's attributes, flavour, selling points, brand character and consumption scene.

Do not apply a fixed circle, square or conventional card template. The custom outline should grow naturally out of "product form + product-name lettering + information blocks + decorative elements", so the whole tag reads as one complete graphic rather than several elements stuck onto a base board. The outline may extend, wrap, turn, offset, protrude locally, carry a sticker-style stroke or layer, but the whole must stay compact, complete and recognisable at a glance.

The product itself is the visual anchor. It may be cut out, break the boundary locally, interleave front and back, or show an enlarged detail, so that product and tag structure truly interlock instead of simply placing a product photo in the middle.

The product name must be the most characterful visual element. Choose intelligently, according to the product's character, handwriting, signboard type, rounded bold, retro type, playful distorted type, brush type, dessert-like type, trendy letterforms or another suitable expression, so the text itself is graphic. Letterforms may rotate slightly, stagger, stretch, overlap, take strokes or shadows, or interleave with the product outline, but must always stay clear and readable, still quickly recognisable when reduced.

Necessary product information should not be laid out mechanically but cleverly integrated into the tag structure. Price, size, flavour, core selling points, main ingredients, recommendation lines, brand name, English name, promotional information and so on can, by importance, naturally become price tags, recommendation stamps, small stickers, corner marks, ribbons, handwritten notes, round seals, curved text, mini info boxes or edge annotations. Every piece of information should feel like part of the design, not explanatory text added afterwards.

A small amount of sticker language may be generated intelligently from the product's characteristics, such as "house signature, manager's pick, popular item, classic flavour, new, limited, handmade, real ingredients, rich, low sugar, molten, freshly baked, must-order". Do not copy these mechanically; judge the product's most worthwhile reason to buy, then generate the most suitable tag content and visual form.

Colour extends from the product itself: product colour, flavour colour, brand colour and accents echo each other. Bright, retro, sweet, rich, fresh or trendy are all allowed, but avoid unfounded piles of colours. Outline, stroke, base colour and local information blocks should help the product jump out of the e-commerce page.

Small product-related illustrations, ingredient graphics, hand-drawn lines, symbols, stars, leaves, fruit, cutlery, taste symbols, curved English text or light decoration may be added to make the information more fun, but in restrained number, never overpowering the product.

The final effect should be like a "self-contained visual badge" that a mature brand designed specifically for this product:
from afar you first see the product name and the product,
then the price or core selling point,
and only up close the flavour, size, ingredients and playful details.

Overall: a creative custom outline, a prominent product, a striking name, complete but uncrowded information, characterful type, clear hierarchy, strong e-commerce click appeal and brand memorability.

Avoid: template rounded cards, product separated from the base board, all information in rectangles, ordinary type, an isolated price, stickers everywhere, evenly distributed elements, a characterless outline, fragmented information, and an unclear product name.
