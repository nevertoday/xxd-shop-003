# 🦁 XXD Shop 003 | Custom-Shaped Product Tag

### An emblem that grows out of the product itself

<a href="README.md">简体中文</a> · <strong>English</strong> · <a href="README.ja.md">日本語</a> · <a href="README.ko.md">한국어</a> · <a href="README.ar.md">العربية</a>

**Make a custom-shaped recommendation badge that grows out of the product — not another rounded card.**
Its outline comes from the product's silhouette, the name's lettering and a few information blocks, so product and name read from across the room.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **You give** | The product name (required) plus a product photo (recommended); price, size, flavour and similar facts |
| **You get** | A badge on a clean plain background, ready for a detail page or in-store print; a transparent version on request |
| **The look** | A custom silhouette · the product interlocking the board · a characterful name · information built into the structure · a few playful marks |
| **Best for** | Detail-page recommendation modules, in-store cards, price tags, new-arrival corners |

> **Once installed, just say:**
>
> /xxd-shop-003 Sea-salt cheese bagel, ¥16 each, 120 g — make a recommendation badge

## What it solves

Detail pages and shelf cards usually look the same: a rounded card, a product photo in the middle, three lines of text. Everything is there, and nobody remembers it.

**Shop 003** does not start from a card template. The badge's outline is set by **the product's silhouette, the name's lettering and a few information blocks**: the product can lean out of the edge, the name can wrap around it, the price can be a small tag, the flavour a ribbon. The result is one shape, not things stuck on a board.

From afar you recognise the product and its name, then the price or key benefit; up close you find the flavour, size and small details.

## What to prepare

- **The product name** (required): the name is part of the outline.
- **A product photo** (recommended): cut-outs, breaking the edge and interlocking all rely on it.
- **Price, size, flavour, ingredients** (recommended): the more precise, the better the information blocks. Without a price there is no price tag and no "¥XX" placeholder.
- **Your own selling copy** (optional): if you give it, yours is used; if not, a draft comes first for you to review.

## How to use it

### Step 1: install (once)

```bash
git clone https://github.com/nevertoday/xxd-shop-003.git
mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills
ln -s "$(pwd)/xxd-shop-003" ~/.claude/skills/xxd-shop-003
```

Codex users link the same folder into `~/.codex/skills/xxd-shop-003`. Restart your AI assistant's session after installing.

### Step 2: say what you want in plain words

```text
/xxd-shop-003 Sea-salt cheese bagel, ¥16 each, 120 g — make a recommendation badge
/xxd-shop-003 Freeze-dried strawberry crisps: whole berries, no added sugar, sweet-tart crunch, 30 g bag
/xxd-shop-003 One 4:5 badge for each product in ~/Desktop/new-skus
```

No flags to memorise. Anything you leave open, such as sizes or how many, is asked in one go, and next time you can reuse your previous settings.

### Step 3: what it does next

1. Reads your product photo and description and asks only what is still open; sizes are recommended from the use, with pixel dimensions.
2. Without your copy, it first drafts the badge text, each line tagged with its source (your description, the packaging or your facts); it generates only after you confirm or edit it.
3. Every size is composed afresh, never cropped from another image.
4. Checks each image before delivery: is the product unchanged, is the text spelled right, are there numbers you never gave? Anything that fails is reported and redone.
5. Saves PNGs to a new task folder under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-shop-003/` and gives you the path.

## FAQ

**What about a transparent background?**

Just ask. The badge is generated on a plain colour and then cut out — never a painted grey-and-white checkerboard.

**Will it add "chef's pick" or "best seller" on its own?**

No. Shoppers read those as real endorsements, so they appear only if you said them. Attribute words like "molten" or "freshly baked" appear only when visible or stated by you.

**What do I need?**

Three things: ① an AI assistant that supports Skills, such as Claude Code or Codex; ② an image-capable model — your assistant's built-in image generation, or a model provider configured in Codex that generates images through the Responses API (GPT Image 2 by default); ③ Python 3.11+ with Pillow (`pip install pillow`).

**Does it cost anything? Can I use it commercially?**

The Skill is free to download; image generation is billed to your own model account. The project uses the PolyForm Noncommercial licence: personal learning, research and testing are fine; running a shop, client work or other commercial use needs the author's written permission first — see "License" at the end.

**How large are the images?**

You can ask for a ratio or exact pixels. The real size is whatever the model returns — never secretly upscaled — and the file name states the true pixels.

**Will the text in the image have typos?**

AI images occasionally misspell words or add small marks such as ® next to a brand name. Every image is zoomed and checked before delivery, and any failure is redone; all samples on this page passed that check.

**Will it invent prices or sales numbers?**

No. Anything that looks like a fact — price, specs, origin, sales, awards, certifications — comes only from what you provide or text clearly readable on the product; otherwise it is left out, with no "¥XX" placeholder.

**Can it handle many products at once?**

Yes. Give it a folder of product photos: each image is one product, sizes and text are asked once, and no product's facts or copy leak into another's.

## Principles

- **The style stays true:** behind this Skill is a mature commercial design prompt ([original brief](references/original-prompt/zh-CN.md) · [English reading translation](references/original-prompt/en.md)). The Skill never rewrites it or adds its own colours or art direction; it only hands the model your product, sizes, text and facts.
- **The product is not redesigned:** with a product photo, shape, colour, packaging text and logo follow the photo.
- **Only your facts,** and each product stands alone — nothing borrowed from other products, earlier results or samples.
- **Real PNG bitmaps,** never SVG or code drawings passed off as images; every run creates a new task folder.
- One badge, one product; a bundle can be named and treated as one product.
